## 1. Executive Summary

CloudBrew Coffee Co. generated **₹15,64,616 in total revenue** across 1,096 transactions from January to June 2026, with an average order value of **₹1,427.57**. The business shows genuine organic growth — Q2 organic revenue was ₹6.5L vs Q1 organic (ex-Pune anomaly) of ₹5.5L, representing ~18% QoQ growth.

However, the audit uncovered **six critical operational and financial red flags** that must be addressed before the Series B close:

| # | Red Flag | Impact |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Pune Bulk-Order Anomaly** | CUST1499 drove 39.9% of Pune's total revenue (56 transactions) — potential channel fraud |
| 2 | **Hyderabad Inventory Crisis** | 77 out of 77 Critical stock events are concentrated in a single store |
| 3 | **Marketing Waste** | Two campaigns (Podcast Sponsorship + Influencer Collab May) destroyed ₹3.36L with near-zero conversions |
| 4 | **Payroll Anomaly** | A Content Creator (EMP122) is paid ₹1,85,000/mo — more than any Regional Manager |
| 5 | **Delhi Store Bleeding** | Only 78 orders in 6 months (lowest of any store), with 40% footfall decline |
| 6 | **Logistics Cost Explosion** | LogiExpress India billed ₹54.5L in 6 months; 15 invoices are overdue totalling ₹51L |

### 5.2 Key Store Observations

**Pune-Viman — Artificially Inflated Leader**
- Tops the revenue table at ₹4,76,691, but 39.9% of that (₹1,90,032) comes from a single customer, CUST1499, across 56 transactions. Many of these involved bulk quantities (e.g., 25 units of Geisha in one order, 16 units of Subscription Box, 18 units of Decaf Smooth). This pattern is highly abnormal and is flagged in the CEO notes as a potential fraud or unregistered channel partnership.

**Bangalore-Koramangala — True Revenue Leader**
- With 256 orders (highest volume) and ₹3.1L in organic revenue, Bangalore is the most consistently active store. ATV (₹1,217) is in line with network average.

**Mumbai-Bandra — High Quality, Low Complaints**
- ₹2.6L in revenue with low support ticket volume and strong NPS. Cited by CEO as a profit engine alongside Bangalore.

**Delhi-Connaught — Critical Underperformer**
- Only 78 orders in 6 months — the lowest by a wide margin, nearly half of Chennai's 141 orders. Revenue share is just 5.7% of the network. CEO confirms 40% footfall decline due to ongoing metro construction near Connaught Place. **Lease renewal decision is due in September 2026.**

**Hyderabad-HiTec — Inventory Crisis Store**
- Revenue is in line with other mid-tier stores, but inventory data shows **all 77 Critical stock events across the entire network are concentrated at this single store**. Items including Ethiopian Yirgacheffe, Colombian Supremo, and Limited Edition Geisha repeatedly hit Critical levels from January onwards.

**Key Insight:** The best ROI campaigns are the cheapest ones (email, WhatsApp, campus outreach). The worst are expensive broadcast/influencer deals. Budget re-allocation from traditional broadcast channels to performance-based digital channels could multiply marketing ROI.

### 9.2 Critical Stock Events — Store Breakdown

**100% of all Critical stock events occurred at Hyderabad-HiTec.**

This is an extraordinary finding — no other store recorded a single Critical stock event, while Hyderabad recorded 77 across the 26-week period. This points to a **systemic replenishment failure** at the Hyderabad outlet.

**Products most affected at Hyderabad-HiTec:**
- Ethiopian Yirgacheffe — Critical from Week 1 (stock=8, well below reorder point of 15)
- Limited Edition Geisha — Critical from Week 1 (stock=8)
- Colombian Supremo — Hit Critical by Week 2
- Monsoon Malabar — Critical by Week 2

**Connection to Support Tickets:** Cross-referencing with support tickets, Hyderabad has 31 tickets (tied with Pune) with **Delivery Delay** as the most common issue type. The inventory shortage is directly causing customer service problems.

**Connection to Supplier Invoices:** Karnataka Coffee Estates (primary single-origin bean supplier) has multiple Overdue invoices (INV5003, INV5016, INV5029, INV5054, INV5066) — payment delays may be causing supply interruptions to Hyderabad.

The bulk-order anomaly from CUST1499 in March created an artificial inventory demand spike at Pune. Products like Cold Brew Concentrate saw 7–8 units ordered in single transactions repeatedly across a 2-week period. This would have triggered unnecessary replenishment orders and distorted demand planning signals.
